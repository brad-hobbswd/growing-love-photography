Meet Samantha

A photographer who understands that comfort matters.

Growing Love Photography was created from a deeply personal place. Samantha is a wife and mama first, and her journey into photography has been shaped by the importance of patience, understanding, and creating an environment where families can simply be themselves.

Her son is on the spectrum, and through him she has learned how meaningful it is when children and families are given room to feel safe, seen, valued, and included.

Here, there is no pressure to perform for the camera. The goal is to capture the connection that is already there and preserve the moments you will want to remember.
